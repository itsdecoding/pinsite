FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

Straight answers to the things people ask before they hire us. If yours is not here, email and we will answer it the same day.

Money and timing

How much does a website cost?

It depends on what the site has to do. A four or five page site for a local business is a very different job from a shop with two hundred products. What stays the same is how we price it: you get a short discovery call, then a fixed written quote. The price you approve is the price you pay. No hourly surprises, no "that was out of scope" invoice at the end.

How long does a project take?

Most sites go live within a week of kickoff once the content is ready. Bigger builds, like a shop with a few hundred products, take longer, and you get a dated timeline before we start. If anything moves it, you hear about it the same day, not at the deadline.

How do payments work?

Half when you approve the proposal, half when the site goes live. Care plans are billed monthly. Everything is on a normal invoice you can pay by card or transfer.

Do you do payment plans?

For bigger projects, yes. Tell us on the discovery call and we will split it into stages that line up with the work.

Working together

What's the difference between design and development?

Design is how the site looks and flows: layout, type, colour, the order things appear in, what a visitor is meant to do next. Development is the code that makes that real in a browser. At a lot of agencies those are two teams and a handoff in between. Here the same people do both, so the thing you approved is the thing that gets built.

What do we need to give you?

Your logo if you have one, any photos or writing you want to keep, and about an hour a week for feedback. If you have no logo or no copy, that is fine too, branding and copywriting are things we do. The only thing we really need from you is quick answers when we ask questions.

Will you write the words?

Yes. Most of our clients hand us rough notes or a phone call and we turn that into the copy. You approve every line before it goes live.

Do you do the photos?

We source stock where it fits and can arrange a photographer for anything that needs to be real, like your team, your premises or your products. Photo costs are quoted separately so you can decide.

Can we see it before it is built?

Always. You get a clickable design to poke at on your phone and laptop before a line of code is written. Changes are cheap at that stage, so that is where we do most of the tweaking.

What if I already have a site?

We look at what is working, keep whatever is genuinely good, and rebuild the rest. Your search rankings and old links get carried across so you do not lose traffic on launch day.

After launch

Who owns the website?

You do. The domain, the code and the content are in your name from day one. If you ever leave, you take everything with you, and we hand over every login we hold.

What happens after launch?

Every project comes with a year of care included. That means updates, small fixes, security patches and a short monthly report in plain English. After the year you can carry on with a care plan or take the keys and run it yourself.

Where is the site hosted?

We set up hosting, the domain, email forwarding and the SSL certificate as part of the build, so nothing is left on your to do list. The accounts are in your name, we just manage them.

What about the legal pages?

Privacy policy, terms and cookie notice come with the site, written for your business, not copied from a template you have to decode. You check the details, we put them live.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. Text, images and blog posts are editable without touching code, and we walk you through it on a short call at handover. Anything bigger, send it to us.

Can't AI just build my website?

AI tools can put out a decent looking page in minutes, and we use them wherever they speed us up. What they cannot do is know your market, your customers, or why a visitor should pick you over the place down the road. That judgement is the job. The tools are just tools.

Still got a question?

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device so it can remember something about your visit. Most sites use a few. This one uses fewer than most.

The cookies we use

CookieWhat it doesLasts
EssentialKeeps the site working, remembers a form you started, stores your cookie choice. Cannot be switched off.Up to 12 months
Analytics ([provider])Tells us which pages get read and roughly where visitors come from, in aggregate. Only set if you accept it.Up to 24 months

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell or share cookie data with advertisers.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log a request to fetch them. Embedded videos, if any, come from YouTube and may set their own cookies when you play them. Their policies apply to those.

How to manage them

You can change your choice at any time using the cookie link in the footer, or delete and block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the essential cookies may stop parts of the site working. Blocking analytics changes nothing for you.

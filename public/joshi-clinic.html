Everything your teeth need, under one roof.

Root canal treatment

Clears infection from inside the tooth and saves it from extraction.

Re-root canal

Re-treats an older root canal that has become painful or infected again.

Caps & crowns

Protects a weak or treated tooth with a cap matched to the teeth beside it.

Dentures

Replaces missing teeth with complete or partial dentures, so you can eat, speak and smile with ease.

Tooth-coloured fillings

Seals a cavity with a filling that matches the natural shade of your tooth.

Chipped tooth repair

Rebuilds a broken front tooth to its natural shape, often in one sitting.

Scaling & polishing

Lifts tartar and stains with a professional cleaning and keeps gums healthy.

Extractions

Removes a tooth that cannot be saved, carefully and without hurry.

Children’s dentistry

Gives children calm first visits, small fillings and preventive care for growing teeth.

Not sure which treatment you need? Book a check-up and Dr. Joshi will explain.

Your visit,
step by step.

What happens from the moment you call to the day your treatment is finished, so nothing comes as a surprise.

  1. 1

    Book a time

    Call or WhatsApp the clinic and pick a morning (10–2) or evening (4–8) slot.

  2. 2

    Examination

    Dr. Joshi checks your teeth and gums, takes an X-ray if needed, and shows you what she finds.

  3. 3

    Your treatment plan

    Dr. Joshi lays out each option and how many sittings it takes, and you choose what suits you.

  4. 4

    Treatment

    Done at a calm pace over the sittings you agreed, with local anaesthesia wherever it is needed.

  5. 5

    Aftercare

    Simple home-care advice and a follow-up check to make sure everything has settled well.
